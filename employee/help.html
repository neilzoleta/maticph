Frequently Asked Questions / Reminders

  • How to file a leave request

    You can file a leave by clicking the 'File Leave' button above, selecting your desired date, and submitting your leave request.

  • When will payroll be credited

    Payroll is usually credited every 15th and 30th of the month. Delays may occur on weekends or holidays.

  • How to update your contact information

    To update your contact information, please submit a ticket via the HR Support Line indicating the changes needed.

  • Who to contact for benefits concerns

    For benefits concerns, you may contact the HR Benefits team through the HR Support Line or email benefits@maticstudio.net.
